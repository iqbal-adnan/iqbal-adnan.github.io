Completed my PhD from Indian Institute of Technology Indore, India. My research focuses on utilizing time grid measurements to ensure efficient and safe operations of the power grid, and implementing advanced GUIs for real-time analytics that providing a user-friendly scalabel performant interface to visualize complex grid data.

Other research interests include data-driven analytics for power systems monitoring, EMT simulations of power systems with integration of converter-based generation.
