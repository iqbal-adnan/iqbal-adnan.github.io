Completed my PhD from Indian Institute of Technology Indore, India. My research focuses on utilizing time grid measurements to ensure efficient and safe operations of the power grid, and implementing advanced GUIs for real-time analytics that providing a user-friendly interface to visualize complex grid data. I am also interested in developing scalable performant software prototypes for grid monitoring.

I am broadly interested in data-driven applications for power systems to infer and predict systems stability, EMT simulations of power systems with integration of converter-based generation.
